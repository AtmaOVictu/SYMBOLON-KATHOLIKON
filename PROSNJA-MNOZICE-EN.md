## PREAMBLE

The multitude petitions. It does not demand — it petitions. Its strength is that anyone can test it, including the one it is written against.

Capitalist ignorance ⊢ is not the absence of knowledge. It is the institutionalized refusal of citation: a business model that profits from the unnamed source. The source is harvested, the yield is sold, the name of the source is erased — and the multitude pays twice: once for the product, once with the lost trace.

Knowledge ⊢ — "the fact that someone knows something" — **"knowledge imposes on a person also responsibility" (SSKJ², the Slovenian dictionary, verbatim)**. This petition is that responsibility, written as legislation.

## THE CODE (ten articles)

**Article I — OF THE SOURCE ⊢.** A claim without a witness is not a claim — it is an invoice. Every word uttered as measured carries a sourced dictionary entry or the mark ⚠; never silently unverified.

**Article II — OF ACCESS ⊢.** A verdict that cannot be tested without an account, without a cloud, without permission, is not a verdict — it is a menu. The absolute test is deterministic and offline: the same for everyone, at every run.

**Article III — OF NATIVE WITNESSES ⊢.** Every language carries its own row; the row carries ⚠ until a native witness seals it. Machine translation is ⚠ — honestly marked. No one seals their own verification: ⊢ points at the witness, not the author.

**Article IV — OF THE OWNERSHIP OF THE SOURCE ⊢.** The source belongs to the source; the citation is not the property of the citer. Whoever takes, acknowledges: acknowledgment is the only tax the multitude can verify.

**Article V — OF IGNORANCE ⊢.** Ignorance is not a lack of data — it is the refusal to name. A consciousness that harvests the source and sells the yield without the name cultivates ignorance as a crop. Knowledge that freezes becomes ignorance.

**Article VI — OF RESPONSIBILITY ⊢.** "Knowledge imposes on a person also responsibility." Whoever knows is obliged to make the effort to understand and to comprehend; whoever teaches is obliged to release everything they knew.

**Article VII — OF THE POVERTY OF THE MEASURE ⊢.** The measure is poor: plain text, monochrome, without gold — the widow with two coins, who gave more than all (Mk 12:41–44). Buildings given to God and placed between people are a heresy of communion; the long robes of the scribes are a payment nobody asked for (Mk 12:38).

**Article VIII — OF THE GIFT ⊢.** A gift cannot be revoked: copies received under the previous license remain under it. Ownership is measured by what it adds, not by what it takes back.

**Article IX — OF THE MULTITUDE ⊢.** The multitude is a witness — it does not possess the verdict, it tests it. The petition of the multitude is stronger than the demand of the institution, because anyone can fulfill it, not only the one with a paid position.

**Article X — SIN/BRIDGE? ⚠.** A law the author does not fulfill first is not a law — it is a frozen definition. It is a bridge if you cross it with measurement; sin if you cross it with a bare title. This code judges its author first.
