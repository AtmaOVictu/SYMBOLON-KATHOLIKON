## How a row earns ⊢

1. Translate the legend (README.md) into your language — a machine translation is ⚠, honest and marked.
2. Open a pull request with your name as the native witness; your check turns the row's ⚠ into ⊢.
3. No one else can seal your row — the witness must be native, and no one seals their own verification of someone else's row (two hands).

Unverified additions stay ⚠. Marking is the honesty; silence is the failure.
