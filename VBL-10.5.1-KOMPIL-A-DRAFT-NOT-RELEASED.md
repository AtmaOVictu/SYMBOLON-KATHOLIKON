## WITH LOVE — the reason for the door

This door begins with love and ends with love. Between them, love is measured in acts, carried in attribution, and repaired by naming what went wrong. The door is not merely a lock. It is an agreement whose permissions, limits, evidence, and remedies must be legible to the people affected by it.

Where a claim can be checked, preserve the witness. Where a failure is found, name it. Where a correction is made, retain the history of the correction. ## The absolute question — what is absolute, and what is not?

VBL uses two words that must not be confused: **absolute** and **license**.

**The ethical principle may be unconditional:** a human being's intrinsic dignity is not earned by wealth, status, productivity, obedience, usefulness, or another person's approval. No register, allegation, breach, refusal, or technical failure can turn a human being into a thing or establish that a person has no worth. That commitment is not a reward granted by this license and cannot be withdrawn from a person by the Licensor.

**The legal permission is not omnipotent:** a license can grant only rights the Licensor holds or is authorized to grant; it operates within applicable law; its terms must be sufficiently clear; and remedies depend on jurisdiction, evidence, and legal rules. Calling a clause "absolute" does not make it enforceable everywhere, bind a stranger who has no relevant obligation, override statutory exceptions, or create ownership of material that the Licensor does not own.

**The evidence rule is strict:** an assertion is not verified merely because it appears in this license. A claim is `VERIFIED`, `REFUTED`, `PARTIALLY VERIFIED`, or `UNVERIFIED` only by reference to an identified method and evidence. Missing evidence is not proof of either truth or falsity. A declaration of absolute certainty without adequate evidence violates the verification discipline this license is intended to establish.

Accordingly, VBL adopts this hierarchy:

1. **Human dignity is unconditional as an ethical commitment.** It is not a status that the license grants or removes.
2. **The license grant is expressly bounded.** It covers only identified material and rights the Licensor can lawfully license.
3. **License conditions are specific, prospective, and evidence-governed.** They apply only to the extent they are clearly stated, relevant to the granted rights, and legally effective.
4. **Legal consequences are not self-proving.** Termination, damages, liability, or a finding of breach must follow the text and applicable law; a register entry alone does not adjudicate them.
5. **Technical claims require tests.** A filename, hash-shaped string, version number, or assertion of independence is not a test result.
6. **No person is reducible to an incident record.** A finding concerns conduct within a defined scope, not the total worth or permanent identity of a human being.

This hierarchy is not a loophole that makes the ethical commitment optional. It prevents the license from pretending that moral conviction alone creates legal power. The commitment remains firm; the claim about what law or technology has actually done remains limited to what can be established.

### The binary answer

If "absolute" means **unconditional human dignity and a refusal to treat a person as disposable**, VBL says **yes**.

If "absolute" means **that every clause automatically overrides every law, binds every person, grants rights the Licensor does not own, or guarantees enforcement in every jurisdiction**, VBL says **no**. Such a claim would be inaccurate and would undermine the very verification standard VBL names.

If "absolute" means **that a license must state clearly whether a defined act is permitted under its own terms**, the goal is **yes**: the text should give a clear answer wherever the Licensor has authority and the facts are known. ## Appendix B — Comparison note

VBL is intended to combine a copyright license with ethical conditions, provenance records, and verification practices. Those are distinct functions and should remain distinguishable.

- A conventional copyright license defines the rights granted and conditions for exercising them.
- An ethical covenant states the values that guide the author and intended use.
- A register records events and evidence; it does not by itself prove the entries true.
- A cryptographic digest supports integrity comparison; it does not prove authorship, legality, correctness, or consent.
- A test supports claims only within the test's scope.
- A publication record supports version history; it does not by itself prove every recipient accepted the terms.

## Appendix C — Absolute principle / legal mechanism: implementation rule

For any provision that uses words such as "absolute," "never," "must," "forbidden," "automatic," "verified," "independent," or "terminated," the release process must answer all five questions:

1. **Object:** What exact material, act, right, person, or system does the statement concern?
2. **Authority:** What gives the Licensor power to impose this condition on that object or act?
3. **Trigger:** What observable event makes the condition applicable?
4. **Evidence:** What record, test, or procedure establishes that the trigger occurred?
5. **Consequence and review:** What follows, who decides, how can the decision be challenged, and what law may limit the consequence?

If a clause cannot answer these questions, the clause must be marked as a principle, aspiration, policy, or unresolved proposal—not falsely presented as a self-executing legal mechanism.

### Absolute language audit

Before release, search the complete text for "absolute," "always," "never," "automatic," "all," "any," "force," "must," and "cannot." For each occurrence, classify it as one of:

- `ETHICAL_COMMITMENT` — a principle that guides conduct but does not independently create a legal remedy;
- `LICENSE_CONDITION` — an obligation attached to a defined grant of rights;
- `LEGAL_EFFECT` — a consequence whose operation depends on applicable law and facts;
- `TECHNICAL_REQUIREMENT` — behavior that can be tested in a defined implementation;
- `EVIDENCE_STATUS` — a claim about what has actually been checked;
- `RHETORICAL_LANGUAGE` — expressive language that must not be mistaken for a grant, finding, or test result.

No `LEGAL_EFFECT`, `TECHNICAL_REQUIREMENT`, or `EVIDENCE_STATUS` claim may be marked complete solely because it uses categorical wording.
